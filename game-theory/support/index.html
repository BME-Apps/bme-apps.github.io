Frequently asked questions

How do I type payoffs and probabilities?

Whole numbers, decimals and fractions all work: −2, 1.5, 1,5, 2/3. Probabilities of the types of one player must add up to exactly 1 (for example 1/3, 1/3, 1/3).

Which kinds of games can the Solver handle?

  • Matrix — 2 players with up to 6 strategies each, or 3 players with up to 3 strategies each; 14 solution concepts.
  • Tree — games where players move one after another, including moves of chance; solved by backward induction and compared with the Nash equilibria.
  • Bayesian — two players with private types; pure and mixed Bayesian Nash equilibria.
  • Cournot — 2 to 5 firms choosing quantities, with known or private costs; compared with a cartel, a Stackelberg leader and perfect competition.

Why do some results show “≈”?

Almost everything is computed with exact fractions. The one numerical step is the fully mixed case of three-player games; those values are marked “≈”.

How do I change the language?

Tap the language button at the top right of the Learn, Solver or Practice screen. The app speaks English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese and Japanese, and starts in your device’s language when it is one of them.

How do I start over?

Each Solver mode has a button to start a new game. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device, including your learning progress.

Does the app collect my data?

No. It works fully offline and keeps your games and progress only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

I found a wrong result.

Thank you — please send us the game (a screenshot of the input is perfect) and what you expected. We check every report.
