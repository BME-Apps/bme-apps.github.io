Frequently asked questions

How do I type payoffs and probabilities?

Whole numbers, decimals and fractions all work: −2, 1.5, 1,5, 2/3. Probabilities of the types of one player must add up to exactly 1 (for example 1/3, 1/3, 1/3).

Which kinds of games can the Solver handle?

  • Matrix — 2 players with up to 6 strategies each, or 3 players with up to 3 strategies each; 14 solution concepts.
  • Tree — games where players move one after another, including moves of chance; solved by backward induction and compared with the Nash equilibria.
  • Bayesian — two players with private types; pure and mixed Bayesian Nash equilibria.
  • Cournot — 2 to 5 firms choosing quantities, with known or private costs; compared with a cartel and perfect competition and, with two firms, a Stackelberg leader.

Why do some results show “≈”?

Almost everything is computed with exact fractions. A value such as 2/3 ≈ 0.667 is an exact fraction followed by its rounded decimal. The one numerical step is the fully mixed case of three-player games; those values are approximations and are also marked “≈”.

How do I change the language?

Tap the language button at the top right of the Learn, Solver or Practice screen. The app speaks English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese and Japanese, and starts in your device’s language when it is one of them.

How do I start over?

Each Solver mode has a button to start a new game. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device, including your learning progress.

What is free, and what does Full Access unlock?

Lessons 1, 2 and 24 and two practice problems (the Prisoner’s Dilemma and Chicken, with their solutions) are free. The Solver’s editors are open to everyone, and without Full Access each solve is unlocked by watching one short ad. Full Access — a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription — unlocks every other lesson and all practice problems with their solutions, and lets you solve without ads.

How do the ads work?

When you press Solve without Full Access, the app offers you a short ad. Ads appear only when you ask for one, and there are no banners. Watch the ad to the end and that game is solved; close it early and this solve stays locked. One ad unlocks one solve, so going back and pressing Solve again needs another ad. Watching an ad needs an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available: please try again a little later. The app is rated 18+ because these ads may include content for mature audiences. From version 1.2.2, iOS may ask before the first ad whether the app can track you across other companies’ apps and websites; the ad plays whatever you answer, and you can change your answer in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. To report an ad, use “Report an inappropriate ad” in the ad window.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, tap any locked item to open the purchase screen and choose “Restore purchase”. Nothing is charged.

I bought Full Access but the content is still locked.

Check that you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with, make sure the device is online, and tap “Restore purchase”. If it is still locked, write to us with the date of the purchase — Apple’s receipt email helps.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the app collect my data?

We receive no personal data from the app, and your games and progress stay only on your device. Lessons, Practice and the Solver’s editors work offline, and so does solving with Full Access. Two things connect to the internet. Once you have chosen to watch an ad, Google’s ad software in the app sends information such as your IP address, device details and an identifier for this app to Google, to show and measure ads and, depending on where you live and on your choices, to personalise them. If you allow tracking, Google and its ad partners can also use your device’s advertising identifier. Purchases connect to Apple’s App Store, to show the price of Full Access and to buy, restore and verify it. See the Privacy Policy.
