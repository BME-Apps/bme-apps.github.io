Privacy Policy

Game Theory: Nash Equilibrium (iOS) · Effective date: 30 October 2026

In short: the app has no accounts and no analytics tools, and we never sell your data. What you enter stays on your device. Without Full Access, you can choose to watch a short ad to solve a game in the Solver; only then does the app contact Google’s ad service (Google AdMob), which receives device and usage information to show and measure that ad (see section 4). The app never shows ads on its own, never shows them to Full Access owners and does not ask to track you across other companies’ apps. Its other connection is to Apple’s App Store, to show the price of Full Access and to process and verify the purchase.

1. Who we are

“Game Theory: Nash Equilibrium” (the “App”) is developed and published by BME Apps, an independent developer based in Türkiye. BME Apps is not a company. You can reach us at bme.support.univ@gmail.com.

2. Information the App stores on your device

To remember your work between sessions, the App saves the following information only on your device:

  • the interface language you chose;
  • the games you are editing in the Solver (payoff tables, game trees, Bayesian games, Cournot markets);
  • your learning progress (topics marked as learned, practice problems attempted or solved).

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else by the App, and we have no way to access it. It is deleted when you delete the App. Like the data of any app, it may be included in the backups you make of your device (for example iCloud Backup), under Apple’s terms.

3. The Full Access purchase

Some content is unlocked by a one-time in-app purchase called Full Access. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details, your name or your email address. The App asks Apple’s StoreKit for the price and for a record, signed by Apple, that Full Access belongs to your Apple Account, and checks that record on your device. It stores on your device only whether Full Access is unlocked. Apple sends us sales reports that show how many purchases were made in which country, but not who made them.

4. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

Without Full Access, each solve in the Solver can be unlocked by watching one short video ad to the end (a “rewarded ad”). Ads appear only after you tap Watch an ad; the App shows no banners and no ads when it starts. The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere). If you never tap that button, or if you own Full Access, the App does not start the ad service at all.

When you do, Google’s software in the App (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) collects, for Google: your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information (for example the device model, operating system version and language), an identifier for this app on your device (the identifier for vendors), and information about the ad (whether it was shown, watched to the end or tapped) together with diagnostic data. Google uses it to deliver and measure the ad, to limit how often you see the same ad and to prevent fraud, and — only where you have agreed — to personalise ads. The App never asks for permission to track you (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency), so it does not give Google your device’s advertising identifier. How Google uses this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form (a certified consent management platform) asks for your choices before the first ad, and without consent only limited, non-personalised ads are shown. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of personalised ads in the same way. You can change your choices at any time with Ad privacy choices in the App’s ad window, and you can reset or limit ad identifiers in your device settings.

We do not receive any of this information about you. Google sends us only totals, such as the number of ads shown and the earnings, by country and day. Google may process the information on servers outside Türkiye and outside your country.

5. Sharing a solution

When you tap Share, the App hands the text of the solution to the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example Messages, Mail or Notes). The privacy policy of the app or service you choose applies to what you send. The App itself does not send anything.

6. Information Apple may provide to us

Apple provides developers with aggregated statistics about their apps (for example the number of downloads). If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may also provide crash reports and usage statistics. We cannot identify you from this information, and it is processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We use it only to find and fix problems.

7. If you contact us by email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever your message contains. We use them only to answer you and to fix the problems you report, based on our legitimate interest in responding to your request (Article 5(2)(f) of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, “KVKK”; Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use them for marketing and do not pass them on to anyone. We delete the conversation when it is no longer needed, and at the latest two years after our last message. We receive email through Gmail, a service of Google, which may store messages on servers outside Türkiye. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

8. Children

The App is suitable for all ages but is not directed primarily at children. We do not collect personal information from anyone. The optional ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences, and they appear only when the user asks for one. If a child writes to us, we use the address only to reply and then delete the conversation.

9. Your rights

We do not hold personal data from the App, so there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data that Google collects when an ad is shown, use Ad privacy choices in the App or contact Google through the pages linked in section 4. For any email conversation with us, you may exercise the rights granted to you by law, including the rights listed in Article 11 of KVKK and Articles 15–22 of the GDPR: to learn whether we hold data about you, to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. Write to the address above; we answer within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu) or with the data protection authority of the country where you live. California residents: we do not sell personal information; for ads, the opt-out described in section 4 applies.

10. Security

The App does not transmit the games you enter or your progress; they never leave your device. Purchase records are exchanged only between your device and Apple over Apple’s encrypted connection, and ad requests only between your device and Google over Google’s encrypted connection. Data stored on your device is protected by iOS and by your device passcode.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. If the App ever starts to collect data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before that version is released. This version adds the optional rewarded ads (section 4).
