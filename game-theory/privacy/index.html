Privacy Policy

Game Theory: Nash Equilibrium (iOS) · Effective date: 2 November 2026

In short: the app has no accounts and no analytics tools of its own, and we never sell your data. What you enter stays on your device; if you use the Apple Watch app, results can also reach your own paired Apple Watch. Without Full Access, you can choose to watch a short ad to solve a game in the Solver; the app contacts Google’s ad service (Google AdMob) only after you choose this, and Google and its ad partners may then collect device and usage information to show, measure and, depending on where you live and on your choices, personalise ads (see section 4). The app never shows ads on its own and never shows them to Full Access owners. From version 1.2.2, iOS may ask before the first ad whether the app can track you across other companies’ apps and websites; the ad plays whatever you answer. Its other connection is to Apple’s App Store, to show the price of Full Access and to process and verify the purchase.

1. Who we are

“Game Theory: Nash Equilibrium” (the “App”) is developed and published by BME Apps, an independent developer based in Türkiye. BME Apps is not a company. You can reach us at bme.support.univ@gmail.com.

2. Information the App stores on your device

To remember your work between sessions, the App saves the following information only on your device:

  • the interface language you chose;
  • the games you are editing in the Solver (payoff tables, game trees, Bayesian games, Cournot markets);
  • your learning progress (topics marked as learned, practice problems attempted or solved).

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else by the App, and we have no way to access it. It is deleted when you delete the App. Like the data of any app, it may be included in the backups you make of your device (for example iCloud Backup), under Apple’s terms.

3. The Full Access purchase

Some content is unlocked by a one-time in-app purchase called Full Access. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details, your name or your email address. The App asks Apple’s StoreKit for the price and for a record, signed by Apple, that Full Access belongs to your Apple Account, and checks that record on your device. It stores on your device only whether Full Access is unlocked. Apple sends us sales reports that show how many purchases were made in which country, but not who made them.

4. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

Without Full Access, each solve in the Solver can be unlocked by watching one short ad to the end (a “rewarded ad”). Ads appear only after you tap Watch an ad; the App shows no banners and no ads when it starts. The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere). If you never tap that button, or if you own Full Access, the App does not start the ad service at all. Once you have tapped it, the App may load the next ad in advance, for example when you press Solve again, until the App has fully closed (not just moved to the background).

When the ad service runs, Google’s software in the App (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) collects, for Google: your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information (for example the device model, operating system version and language), an identifier for this app on your device (the identifier for vendors), and information about the ad (whether it was shown, watched to the end or tapped) together with diagnostic data. Google uses it to deliver and measure the ad, to limit how often you see the same ad and to prevent fraud, and, as described under “Your choices” below, to personalise ads. Google and the other ad companies that work with it (its ad partners) may place and read cookies, and may use web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers, to collect information as a result of serving an ad. Google may share information with these partners, and they may use it under their own policies to show and measure ads and, as described under “Your choices” below, to personalise them. How Google uses this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Tracking. From version 1.2.2, after you tap Watch an ad and before the first ad, iOS asks whether the App may track you across other companies’ apps and websites (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, Google’s ad software can read your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), and Google and its ad partners may combine it with information about you from other companies’ apps and websites to show and measure ads. If you do not allow it, they do not get that identifier, but Google still receives the other information described above, including the identifier for vendors. The ad and your solve work the same whatever you answer.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form (a certified consent management platform) asks for your choices before the first ad. Ads are personalised only with your consent; without it only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of personalised ads with Ad privacy choices. Elsewhere, including Türkiye, the App does not ask for these choices, and Google and its ad partners may personalise ads under their own policies.

Where Ad privacy choices applies, it is in the App’s ad window. You can use it after you have tapped Watch an ad and that ad has closed or could not load; if the window closed with the ad, press Solve to open it again. You can change your choices there. After the App has fully closed, the link stays hidden until you next tap Watch an ad.

You can change your tracking answer at any time in your device’s Settings, under Privacy & Security > Tracking. If Allow Apps to Request to Track is off there, the App does not ask and counts as not allowed. If you decline on Google’s consent form, the App does not ask about tracking; if you later consent with Ad privacy choices, it asks then. These are separate choices: changing one does not change the other. The identifier for vendors changes if you delete all of our apps from your device and later reinstall one.

We do not receive any of this information about you. Google sends us only totals, such as the number of ads shown and the earnings, by country and day. Google may process the information on servers outside Türkiye and outside your country.

5. Sharing a solution

When you tap Share, the App hands the text of the solution to the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example Messages, Mail or Notes). The privacy policy of the app or service you choose applies to what you send. The App itself does not send anything.

6. Information Apple may provide to us

Apple provides developers with aggregated statistics about their apps (for example the number of downloads). If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may also provide crash reports and usage statistics. We cannot identify you from this information, and it is processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We use it only to find and fix problems.

7. If you contact us by email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever your message contains. We use them only to answer you and to fix the problems you report, based on our legitimate interest in responding to your request (Article 5(2)(f) of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, “KVKK”; Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use them for marketing and do not pass them on to anyone. We delete the conversation when it is no longer needed, and at the latest two years after our last message. We receive email through Gmail, a service of Google, which may store messages on servers outside Türkiye. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

8. Children

The App is rated 18+ on the App Store because the optional ads may include content for mature audiences. It is not directed at children, and the App sends us no personal information about anyone. Ads appear only when the user asks for one, and any ad can be reported with Report an inappropriate ad in the App. If a child writes to us, we use the address only to reply and then delete the conversation.

9. Your rights

We do not hold personal data from the App, so there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data that Google collects when an ad is shown, use Ad privacy choices in the App where it applies (see section 4), or contact Google through the pages linked in section 4. For any email conversation with us, you may exercise the rights granted to you by law, including the rights listed in Article 11 of KVKK and Articles 15–22 of the GDPR: to learn whether we hold data about you, to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. Write to the address above; we answer within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu) or with the data protection authority of the country where you live. California residents: we do not sell personal information; for ads, the opt-out described in section 4 applies.

10. Security

The App does not transmit the games you enter or your progress to us or to anyone else; they stay on your device, and the results of a solve may be passed to your own paired Apple Watch. Purchase records are exchanged only between your device and Apple over Apple’s encrypted connection. Ad requests go from your device to Google over an encrypted connection, and an ad’s content may come from the servers of Google or of the ad companies that work with it. Data stored on your device is protected by iOS and by your device passcode.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. If what the App collects changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before that version is released. The version of 30 October 2026 added the optional rewarded ads (section 4); this version describes ad partners, cookies and similar technologies, and your choices in more detail.
